# Lecture 3: Realism
## What are theories for?
- Theories are for:
	- describing "reality"
	- explaining phenomenon
	- predicting the future
	- influencing policy
- but they have limits, a combination of their outputs is needed
## Realism (dominant paradigm)
### Key Assumptions
- IR is a struggle for power
- this is due to human nature
- behavior and power is based on material capabilities
- sovereign states are the only thing that matter, the UN matters little
- major states are the only concern
- presents itself as *morally and ideologically neutral* by arguing against politics should maintain conventional standards of morality
### Classical Realism
- humans are selfish and we cannot change that
- because states are selfish, they compete for power
- peace is only temporary
