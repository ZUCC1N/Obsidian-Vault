## Hegemony
- involves coercing people not only by thinking it is in their interests, but also was their idea
- funneled through culture
	- culture is the shared symbolic and emotional content of a community
	- invokes *meaning*
	- Antonio Gramsci
		- early 20th century Italian communist
		- jailed for activism in 1926, where he wrote the prison notebooks
		- questioned orthodox Marxism, and why people did not rebel?
		- Hegemony is a kind of domination that revolves around producing cultural and ideological ideas that normalize and legitimize the dominator'
		- people are coerced to consent, leading them to think it to be common sense with no alternative
		- is both the process and end goal
	- do we always know when/how/why we are being oppressed?
		- no, internalization
