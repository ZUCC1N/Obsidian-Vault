## Orthodox criteria
- arguments are true or false, valid or invalid, sound or unsound
	- soundness: deductive validity + true premises
	- unsound arguments are thus deductive invalid (conclusion does not follow from premises) or arguments with false premises
## Argument Evaluation
- sound arguments do not necessarily mean good (premises directly justify conclusion by arguing for itself and unsound arguments can still be convincing)
- need unorthodox criteria
- **cogency not soundness**
	- An argument in which the premises are rationally acceptable and also properly connected to the conclusion.  
(Govier)
