>P1. It is possible to act in a way to save another person's life [Prem; provisional]
>P2. it is possible to act in a way to increase your own pleasure [prem; provisional]

>P6. most people would not forgo leisure to save another person's life in a far away land [prem; appeal to common knowledge]
>C. Consequentialism must be false [P5, P6; Contradiction]
